2

3

Questions

Short answers. Ask the rest on Reddit.

Does it work in Gaming Mode?

Yes. Add Shotport as a non-Steam game once and launch it from your library. It keeps running in the background.

Do I need to install anything on my phone?

No. It's a web page on your home network. Add it to your home screen and it behaves like an app.

Is it safe?

Everything stays on your wifi. v1 adds a pairing code so nobody else on the network can see your shots.

Why not just use Steam Cloud?

Because uploading to Steam, opening the web, finding the shot and downloading it again is four steps. Shotport is one tap.
